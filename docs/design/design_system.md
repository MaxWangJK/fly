# Design System

Fly is clean, modern, calm, focused, efficient, minimal, information-rich and low-fatigue.

## Tokens

- Palette: cool blue-gray canvases and surfaces, `#4267E8` accent, semantic success/warning/danger and four restrained quadrant colors
- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 and 48
- Radius: 6, 10 and 14; productivity surfaces avoid oversized pills
- Metrics: 232 sidebar, 72 rail, 372 detail panel and 52 task row
- Breakpoints: compact below 760, medium 760–1179, expanded from 1180
- Motion: 120, 200 and 280 ms
- Icons: Material symbols at 16, 20 and 24

## Signature

The same task visibly flows through List, Calendar and Matrix. A compact two-stroke Fly mark anchors navigation, while color remains functional: accent for selection, date and action; quadrant color for classification.

System, light and dark themes share layout and semantic roles. There are no large gradients, glass surfaces, neon effects or decorative backgrounds.
