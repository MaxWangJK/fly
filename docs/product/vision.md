# Product Vision

Fly helps an individual decide what to do, when to do it, and why it matters without requiring an account or network connection.

Its defining experience is one task moving coherently through three perspectives:

- Todo answers “what is actionable?”
- Calendar answers “when?”
- Eisenhower Matrix answers “how important and urgent?”

Fly prioritizes calm density, immediate local persistence, predictable keyboard/mouse behavior, and durable ownership of personal data.
